PartSelect Parts Assistant

AI engineering case study · Feb 2026

A chat agent that diagnoses refrigerator and dishwasher problems and recommends genuine OEM parts from PartSelect.com, with a 6-tool agentic loop on the Claude API.

  • A rule-based pre-classifier detects intent and pre-fetches data before the model call, removing a round trip and cutting time-to-first-token by about 40%.
  • Unknown model numbers are scraped live and cached in Supabase, growing coverage from 2 seeded models to thousands of Whirlpool appliances.
  • Hybrid Chroma semantic search plus keyword fallback, image and PDF uploads, and layered safeguards against invented parts or prices.
  • FastAPI
  • React
  • Claude API
  • Supabase
  • Chroma
